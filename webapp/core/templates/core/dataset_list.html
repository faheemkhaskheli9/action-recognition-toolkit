How this works

Training a custom action-recognition model, start to finish:

  1. Raw footage with more than one person in frame (a restaurant, warehouse, retail floor, anything)? Run Extract tracks first — it detects and tracks every person and crops one clip per tracked person into data/tracks/<video>/. Already have clips trimmed to one person each? Skip straight to labeling.
  2. Label every clip with whatever action taxonomy fits your scenario (staff tasks, customer behavior, anything you define) — upload and label right here on this page, or use the Label page to go through unlabeled clips one at a time.
  3. Review manifest any time to fix or delete labels you've already saved.
  4. Train a model once you've got labeled clips across at least two classes.
  5. Inference a finished checkpoint on a new video — tick "scene mode" if that video also has multiple people, to get a separate prediction per tracked person instead of one label for the whole clip.

Upload videos, see what's in your dataset, and fix labels — all in one place. Prefer labeling one at a time? Use the Label page.
